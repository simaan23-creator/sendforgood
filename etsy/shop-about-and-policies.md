## About section — story

```
At our own wedding, the videographer my husband hired never showed up.
The photos came out beautiful — but the voices, the toasts, the messages
from the people we love? Gone. You can't reshoot a wedding.

SealTheDay is what we built so no couple has to feel that. It's a private
digital time capsule: friends and family record short video messages from
their phones (no app, no accounts), the couple adds photos that didn't
make the album, and the whole vault seals shut — until their first
anniversary, when they open it together.

We're a tiny two-person team in New York. Every order is delivered
automatically within minutes, and a real human answers every message.
```

## FAQ entries (if using Etsy FAQ section)

**When do I get my order?**
Within minutes, by email, 24 hours at the very most. Check spam if you
don't see it — or message us here and we'll resend it.

**Is this a physical item?**
No — nothing ships. You receive a claim link that sets up the couple's
private online vault. That's what makes it perfect for last-minute gifts.

**Do the couple's guests need to download anything?**
No. Guests click a link and record straight from their phone browser.
The couple manages the vault from one free account.

**How long do they keep the videos?**
Forever. Lifetime download access is included — every video and photo
can be saved to their own devices.
